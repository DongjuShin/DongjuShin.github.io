About

SDL · KAIST

Dongju Shin is a Ph.D. candidate at the Smart Design Lab at KAIST, advised by Prof. Namwoo Kang. Dongju is also a co-founder of Narnia Labs and works there as an AI and mechanical engineering researcher and project manager.

Dongju’s research builds deep generative models that create new engineering designs and neural surrogate models that predict how those designs will perform, from a single stiffness value up to a full 3D stress or temperature field. This work has been applied in industry projects with Hyundai, Hyundai Mobis, BMW, Volkswagen, Kumho Tire and Donghee Industrial, on parts such as wheels, cast ribs, car hoods, battery pack joints, lamps and air vents, as well as in crash analysis.

02

Research

Generate candidates, predict how they behave, then choose the trade-off that fits.

Generative AI

Deep Generative Design

Producing diverse, high-performing 2D/3D designs from a handful of references with VAEs, diffusion models and implicit neural representations, then turning them into CAD automatically.

Predictive AI

3D Field Prediction

Neural surrogate models that stand in for expensive CAE. They predict stress, temperature and crash responses as single values, time series or full 3D fields, with uncertainty quantification and multi-fidelity learning.

Optimization

Topology Optimization × ML

Using topology optimization as a data engine for learning structurally efficient patterns, and reviewing how machine learning and deep learning are changing the field.

Human-centered

Design Preference & Aesthetics

Learning what people find appealing through design-feature extraction and weighted ensembles, and trading aesthetics off against engineering performance.
